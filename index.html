PART 1 Frequency & Hertz

  • ✦ Frequency: The number of vibration (complete cycles) per second.
  • ✦ Hertz (Hz): The unit used to measure frequency.
  • ✦ Human Hearing Range: Approximately 20 Hz to 20,000 Hz.

Quick Presets & Scientific Reference

💡 Did You Know?

As humans age, the tiny microscopic hair cells (stereocilia) inside the inner ear cochlea get damaged by loud noises and aging. The cells that detect high-pitch frequencies are at the entrance of the cochlea, meaning they wear out first. This permanent loss is called presbycusis.
